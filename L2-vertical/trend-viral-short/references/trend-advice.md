# Trend advice — situation branches

Companion detail for SKILL.md §0. Keep user-facing copy modest when data is missing.

## Situation copy (examples)

### `new_advertiser`

- EN: “You’re early — prioritize 2–3 very different hooks so you learn what resonates.”
- ZH: 「还在起步：优先测 2–3 个差异大的钩子方向，方便对比。」

### `active_possible_fatigue`

- EN: “If creatives feel repetitive while ads are live, refresh with a clearly different hook/scene — this is qualitative, not an account metric.”
- ZH: 「若在投且感觉素材重复，可换差异更大的钩子/场景。这是定性建议，不是账户疲劳诊断。」

**Never:** “素材已严重疲劳”, invented CTR/ROAS drops.

### `silent_restart`

- EN: “Coming back after a pause — reopen with a trend-tied hook plus one ‘safe’ product-hero direction.”
- ZH: 「沉默后重启：一条贴趋势的钩子 + 一条稳妥的产品英雄方向。」

### `unknown`

- EN: “I don’t have flight data — here’s category-sensible directions from your trend words; tell me if you’re live.”
- ZH: 「暂无投放数据：按你给的趋势词 + 品类常识给方向；若在投请告诉我。」

## Direction quality bar

Each of the 2–3 directions must answer:

1. What is the **hook** in the first second?
2. What **scene** do we show?
3. Why is it tied to the **trend word / format** (or category ritual if no trend word)?
4. Default deliverable: image batch vs short video
